# Illustrative figures only

No `paths` frontmatter, deliberately: it covers issues and PRs as well as files.

This repo is public, and the app runs on the household's real bank data. Everything written here is published: code, comments, tests, docs, commit messages, issues, PR descriptions and review comments.

When real data proves a point, describe it with made-up figures of a similar size, and say they're illustrative: "a card payment of about $6,000 swings spending negative".

Real data stays in the session. That covers:

- amounts, balances, net worth, and row counts
- payees, employers, schools and institutions tied to the household, including raw bank descriptions
- account names and masks
- pasted query results, screenshots and page snapshots

Before publishing anything measured on the live app or database, reread it for these. A test fixture gets a made-up value, even when it reproduces a real bug.

Figures already in the repo were replaced with made-up ones on 2026-11-02, so treat them as illustrative wherever the wording around them still sounds measured.
